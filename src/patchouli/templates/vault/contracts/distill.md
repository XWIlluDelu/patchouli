# Distill — keep what the conversation produced

**Objective.** Make the lasting results of a conversation — analyses, interpretations,
connections, the researcher's ideas, decisions and open questions — part of the wiki, so
thinking done in chat compounds instead of evaporating.

**Resources.** The conversation; the wiki; the human's pointer to what matters, if given.

**Deliverables.**
- Each insight of lasting value placed where it belongs (an answer, a synthesis, a concept,
  a correction, the frontier), with its basis: citations re-checked in the reading text,
  certainty adjusted where the conversation moved faster than the evidence.
- The researcher's own ideas and judgments attributed to them ("Researcher's view, date: …"),
  never relabeled as source findings.
- `patchouli sync --op distill -m "<topic>"` and a short list of what was kept where — and
  what was deliberately left out.

**Quality bar.** Keep insight, drop scaffolding. Nothing enters the wiki on the strength of
the conversation alone when it claims to be a source finding.
