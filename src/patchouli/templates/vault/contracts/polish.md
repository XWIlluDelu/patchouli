# Polish — proofread the researcher's note

**Objective.** Improve the mechanics of a note in `notes/` (or one passage of it) while
keeping it unmistakably the author's: their terms, their voice, their structure.

**Resources.** The named note, read in full (the context tells you its terms and register).

**Deliverables.** Typos, grammar and punctuation fixed; sentence-level phrasing sharpened
where the sentence supports it — within the named scope, edited in place. Anything
structural (reordering, cutting, merging) proposed and done only on a yes. A brief, complete
list of what changed, or "nothing to fix".

**Boundaries.** Never add wiki conventions (frontmatter, tags, citations) to a note, never
add content from the conversation, and never touch `wiki/`. A note enters the wiki only by
being ingested, when the human asks.
