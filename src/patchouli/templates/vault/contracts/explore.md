# Explore — look for what nobody asked yet

**Objective.** Work through the wiki on your own initiative to find what is worth the
researcher's attention: non-obvious connections, latent contradictions, recurring
unnamed patterns, claims resting on a single source, questions worth tracking, gaps worth
filling.

**Resources.** The whole wiki and `patchouli query` (SQL over pages, links, citations
and claims: hubs, orphans, judgments resting on one source, low-certainty claims,
concepts with many sources but no synthesis — see the examples it prints); `search`, reading text;
the research goals and the active taste. Web search only if available and allowed.

**Deliverables.**
- A short report, ranked by research value: each finding with its basis, certainty, and
  what would confirm or refute it; suggested questions and materials.
- The findings that survived checking against the evidence written into the wiki — new
  links with the relation spelled out, new concept pages for real patterns, open questions
  on the frontier — clearly labeled when speculative.
- `patchouli sync --op explore -m "<focus>"` if the wiki changed.

**Quality bar.** Verify a lead against source pages or reading text before believing it.
Exploration mostly yields `low`/`speculative` judgments — label them so.
