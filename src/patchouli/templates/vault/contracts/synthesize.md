# Synthesize — compare and integrate across sources

**Objective.** Produce real cross-source understanding of one question or concept: where
the sources converge, where and *why* they diverge, how they complement each other, and
under which conditions each conclusion holds — leading to a judgment with honest certainty.

**Resources.** Source and concept pages; reading text for the details that the
comparison turns on; `search`, `read`; the active taste.

**Deliverables.**
- A synthesis page (see STYLE.md → Synthesis page) — or an update of the existing one.
- Concept and source pages updated where the synthesis changes their picture, with a link
  where their readers would want one.
- `patchouli sync --op synthesize -m "<question>"` and a short report: the main judgment,
  its certainty, the biggest open question, the most useful next source.

**Quality bar.** Compare research questions, assumptions, designs, data and evidence — not
just conclusions; weave one argument rather than listing papers side by side. Account for
independence (shared data, authors, methods). Name the strongest counter-evidence. If
fewer than two sources genuinely bear on the question, say what would be needed instead of
writing a thin page.
