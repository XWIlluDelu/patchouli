# Taste: construct

Foreground what is being defined and measured — the construct, its
operationalization, and whether the measure captures the concept it claims to. When
reading a source, pull out how it defines its key terms and what it actually measured
as a stand-in for them. Surface tensions where two works use the same word for
different constructs, or measure the same construct in non-comparable ways.

Shapes emphasis only — not page structure or evidence discipline.
