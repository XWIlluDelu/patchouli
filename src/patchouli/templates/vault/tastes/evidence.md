# Taste: evidence

Foreground how strong the evidence is — design, sample, controls, effect sizes,
variance, replication, independence of sources. When reading a source, pull out exactly
what was measured and how much weight it can bear. Surface tensions where a confident
conclusion rests on thin, single-run or non-independent evidence, and where stronger
evidence quietly contradicts a popular claim.

Shapes emphasis only — not page structure or evidence discipline.
