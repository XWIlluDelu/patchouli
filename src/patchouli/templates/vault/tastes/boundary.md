# Taste: boundary

Foreground where a claim holds and where it breaks — scope conditions, assumptions,
generalization limits, failure modes. When reading a source, pull out the conditions
under which its result is true and the conditions it never tested. Ask what would make
the finding fail. Surface tensions where one work's result is another work's
out-of-scope case, and keep claims inside the population, method and context that
earned them.

Shapes emphasis only — not page structure or evidence discipline.
